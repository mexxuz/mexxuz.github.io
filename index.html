Boom & Bust

Boom & Bust is a YouTube documentary channel about American brands: how they rose, and why they fell. Every video is researched from public reporting, and every number is listed with its source in the video description.

Watch on YouTube: youtube.com/@TheBoomAndBust

Boom and Bust Uploader

Boom and Bust Uploader is a private, internal tool used only by the owner of the Boom & Bust channel. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload the channel's own finished videos, set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions and scheduled publish time. It is not offered to other users and does not access any other YouTube channel.
